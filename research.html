Proactive Detection and Forecasting of Bulk Refund Booking Risk

Research Partner · SR

Behavioral Logs

In a large-scale Korean railway ticketing system, Bulk Refund Booking (BRB) occurs when users strategically reserve multiple tickets and later refund unused bookings for personal gain. This behavior distorts demand signals: seats may physically remain available but appear sold out to other passengers, while late refunds can leave operators unable to resell the released inventory.

I analyze large-scale railway booking and refund histories to detect emerging BRB risks early, estimate when those risks may materialize, and provide behavior-based explanations that support timely review, policy action, and seat-inventory recovery.

Research Progression. This research has progressed from proactive user-level BRB detection to a time-to-event formulation that estimates both the likelihood of future risk and the remaining time available for operational intervention.
