Proactive Bulk Refund Booking Detection and Forecasting

Research Partner · SR

Personalized Depression Detection from Multimodal Passive Sensing

Multimodal Passive Sensing

This research models personalized depression risks by comparing an individual’s current behavioral patterns with their historical contexts derived from multimodal passive sensing data.

Related Research

  • BRIDGE — Personalized depression risk analysis through semantic retrieval of behavioral histories
